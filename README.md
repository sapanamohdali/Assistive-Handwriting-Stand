
# ✍️ Assistive Handwriting Support Stand

## 📌 Project Overview

An assistive product design project focused on developing a handwriting support stand for children with disabilities.

The concept aims to provide better writing stability, comfort and support during handwriting activities.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Support children during handwriting activities.
- Improve writing stability.
- Explore accessible and inclusive product design.
- Develop a user-friendly assistive device.
- Address practical challenges through engineering design.

## ✨ Project Highlights

- Assistive handwriting support concept.
- Focus on accessibility and usability.
- Physical support mechanism.
- User-centred design approach.

## 🛠️ Project Domain

- Product Design
- Assistive Technology
- Prototyping
- Mechanical Design
- Accessibility

## 📚 Learning Outcomes

- Understanding assistive technology.
- User-centred product development.
- Engineering design principles.
- Problem-solving and prototyping.
